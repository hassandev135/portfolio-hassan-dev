## Problem

A boutique retailer was losing customers to a slow, template-based store.
Pages took seconds to load, search was unreliable, and the checkout felt
clunky on mobile. They needed a storefront that was fast, searchable, and a
pleasure to use — without migrating away from their existing catalog.

Goals:

- Sub-second navigation between products.
- **Instant** search that tolerates typos.
- A checkout that never loses the cart.

## Approach

Verdant is a headless storefront built on Next.js, pulling catalog data through
a thin API layer and handling payments with Stripe.

- **Instant search** — Algolia powers typo-tolerant, as-you-type results with
  faceted filtering.
- **Optimistic cart** — add-to-cart updates render immediately and sync in the
  background, persisted so a refresh never empties the basket.
- **Performance budget** — images are optimized and lazy-loaded, and the
  critical path is kept lean to protect the Lighthouse score.
- **Accessible checkout** — a single, keyboard-friendly flow with clear
  validation and error states.

## Result

- A **100 Lighthouse performance** score on product pages.
- Search results appear in **under 100ms** as the shopper types.
- Cart abandonment dropped after the checkout redesign.
